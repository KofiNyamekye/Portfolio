Evemed Diagnostics

Overview

A clinic that never closes

Evemed Diagnostics offers ultrasound scans at its clinic and at patients’ homes, around the clock. It needed a website that turns a search into a booked scan.

I planned the structure, set the visual direction and built the site end to end using AI-assisted workflows for page structure, copy, code and SEO — reviewing and refining every page myself.

The challenge

Found, trusted, booked — on a phone

Patients needed to understand the clinic’s services, location, mobile home-visit service and booking options quickly — usually on a phone, and often on slow or patchy mobile data.

The clinic also wanted to be visible on Google and in AI assistants, and to show the real team and process to build trust before a first visit.

My approach

One clear action on every page

  1. Structure & information architectureTen pages on clean URLs: Home, Services, three service groups (pregnancy, abdomen & pelvis, specialised), Mobile services, Our work, About, FAQ and Contact.
  2. Booking flowEvery call to action opens WhatsApp with a pre-written message, plus a short form that builds the message for the patient.
  3. TrustFour work videos and team portraits, hosted on Cloudinary and loaded only when a visitor presses play.
  4. Search & AI visibilityPer-page titles and previews, structured data for the clinic, hours, team, FAQs and videos, a sitemap, crawler rules and an llms.txt, with Google and Bing verification.
  5. ResilienceLoading, offline and slow-network states, a service worker for repeat visits, and Add to Home Screen through a web app manifest.

The result

A site that works like the clinic does

A multi-page healthcare website, live on the clinic’s own domain, with clear service information, mobile home-visit support and direct WhatsApp booking from every page.

WhatsApp-first booking

Patients book without filling in a long form.

Mobile home service

A dedicated page for patients who cannot travel.

Trust through video

Real people and process, without slowing the page.

Built to be found

Structured data and crawler rules for Google, Bing and AI assistants.

Works on any connection

Offline banner with tap-to-call and slow-connection notices.

Installable

Opens from the home screen like an app.
