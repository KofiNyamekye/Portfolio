The anthem story

Released June 2026 ahead of the FIFA World Cup, "All for One, One for All" captures the spirit of unity, resilience and the beautiful game for every nation on Earth. The project combines AI music generation, cinematic storytelling, visual branding, AI character creation, promotional strategy and digital distribution. Key change from C major to D major on the final chorus lifts the emotional peak. The 3D animated Kofi Nyamekye character — a young Black Ghanaian male in the Ghana Black Stars Puma gold jersey — anchors the visual identity across all 30 music video scenes.

// production stack

Tools & workflow

Claude — Anthropic
Creative Director & Production Architect

Wrote full lyrics, developed the musical blueprint, chord progressions, melody direction, BPM, arrangement notes, video prompts, artist biography, distribution metadata, social captions, release strategy and promotional copy.

Suno AI
Music Composer & Producer

Generated the full 5-minute 51-second Afropop anthem with vocals, instrumentation, percussion, brass and choir arrangements — all from structured creative direction.
Veo 3 — Google
Cinematic Video Generator

Generated key cinematic scenes including artist performance shots, final accomplishment scenes and high-quality cinematic clips from text scene prompts.

OpenArt
3D Character & Scene Generator

Generated the stylized 3D animated Kofi Nyamekye character with consistent Ghana Black Stars jersey, microphone and visual identity across all 30 music video scenes.

ChatGPT — OpenAI
Visual Art Director

Image generation used for cover art, release flyers, countdown visuals and the YouTube thumbnail for the official music video.

Clipchamp — Microsoft
Video Editor & Final Assembly

Assembled all generated scenes, synced the audio, added warm cinematic grading, title cards and exported the final YouTube music video.
