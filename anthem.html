All for One, One for All — World Cup Anthem

A fully AI-produced World Cup anthem conceived, directed, and released independently by Kofi Nyamekye. From lyrics to melody direction, cinematic music video to cover art and global distribution — every element was built using AI tools under complete human creative direction.

Artist: Kofi Nyamekye Genre: Afropop / World Music Duration: 5m 51s Released: June 5, 2026
Watch Official Music Video

Project Overview

Released ahead of the FIFA World Cup, the anthem captures the spirit of unity, resilience, and the beautiful game for every nation on Earth. The project combined AI music generation, cinematic storytelling, visual branding, AI-assisted character creation, promotional strategy, and digital media production into one unified release.

AI Production Workflow

CLAUDE (Anthropic) Creative Director and Production Architect. Developed lyrics, musical blueprint, arrangement notes, video prompts, release strategy, captions, and promotional copy.
SUNO AI Music Composer and Producer. Generated the full Afropop anthem including vocals, instrumentation, brass, percussion, and choir arrangements.
VEO 3 (Google) Cinematic Video Generator used for performance scenes and cinematic animated sequences.
OPENART Generated the stylized 3D Kofi Nyamekye character and supporting scenes across the music video.
CHATGPT (OpenAI) Visual Art Director for cover art, flyers, promotional visuals, and YouTube thumbnail creation.
CLIPCHAMP Final video assembly, syncing, cinematic grading, typography, and YouTube export.
