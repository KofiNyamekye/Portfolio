About Me

Engineering foundation, data mindset, AI curiosity, and creative execution

I'm Nyamekye Samuel Asamoah, a Graduate Materials Engineer from Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology with advanced training in Occupational Health and Safety Management based on ISO 45001 from Imperial College of Mines and Safety.

I work at the intersection of engineering, data, artificial intelligence, and design. My focus is on using data-driven and AI-powered solutions to improve industrial operations, safety systems, learning experiences, and digital communication.

At the Electricity Company of Ghana, I supported field operations by resolving faulty meter cases, managing operational data, validating readings from retired and replaced meters, and assisting with service processes. This strengthened my ability to work with real-world systems, apply safety standards, and identify opportunities for efficiency and reliability.

I also bring a creative edge through graphic design, technical training, and content development. As a Microsoft Word tutor at AYEN, I trained over 30 learners. With CodeDevPay Academy, I have worked on course materials for Microsoft Word, Canva, Generative AI, and Content Creation with ChatGPT.
