Process

From first message to launch.

A simple, transparent process, so you always know what happens next.

  1. Discover

    We talk through your goals, audience, timeline and budget, and agree a clear scope before work starts.

  2. Direct

    I set the structure and creative direction: a page plan, a script and storyboard, a moodboard or the questions the data must answer.

  3. Build

    Design, AI production or development, with check-ins along the way so you see progress early and steer it.

  4. Launch

    Final files or a live site, tested on mobile, with a handover so you know how to use and update what you have.

FAQ

Questions clients ask.

Do you work with clients outside Ghana?

Yes. I’m based in Ghana and work remotely with clients anywhere — calls, feedback and handover all happen online.

What do you need from me to start?

A short description of what you need, who it’s for and anything you already have: a logo, photos, copy or examples you like. The project form covers it in a few minutes.

How is pricing worked out?

Each project is quoted on its scope. The budget ranges on the contact form are a starting point for the conversation, not fixed prices.

Can you work with what I already have?

Yes. Redesigning an existing site, refreshing a brand, or turning your photos, footage or spreadsheets into something new are all common starting points.
