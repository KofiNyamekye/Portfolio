Case study

EnglishPLAY

A complete 60-second advert for a premium English-immersion platform that connects children with native English-speaking “playsisters” — produced entirely with AI tools, with no filming or studio costs.

Overview

A broadcast-style ad without a shoot

EnglishPLAY needed a warm, premium advert that shows how children learn English through play. I led the creative strategy, script, visuals and edit, using AI tools at every stage under my direction.

The challenge

Premium feel, zero production budget

A traditional shoot would mean casting children, locations, a crew and a studio. The goal was the same emotional, polished result — with captions in Turkish for the target audience — without any of those costs.

My approach

A directed AI production pipeline

  1. Concept & scriptBrand analysis, ad concept, scene-by-scene storyboard, voiceover script and music direction developed with Claude.
  2. Key visualsHero playground scenes and story moments generated with Gemini from those prompts.
  3. FootageCinematic scenes with synchronised audio generated in Veo 3 from text scene prompts.
  4. Edit & captionsAssembled, timed and captioned in Turkish in Clipchamp, then exported for distribution.

The result

60 seconds, ready to run

A finished 60-second advert, ready for social and digital channels, delivered without a camera, actor or studio session.
