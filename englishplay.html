AI-Powered Video Ad Production

EnglishPLAY — Premium English Immersion Ad Campaign

Conceptualized and produced a full 60-second video advertisement for EnglishPLAY, a Turkish premium English immersion platform that connects children with native English-speaking playsisters for play-based language learning.

Role: Creative Director & AI Producer Project Type: AI-Powered Video Ad Production Duration: 60 seconds Zero traditional filming or studio costs
Watch Video

Project Overview

The campaign was built entirely using AI tools from creative strategy and scripting through to video generation and post-production, with zero traditional filming or studio costs.

Tools & Production Workflow

Claude AI — AnthropicPrimary creative engine for brand analysis, ad concept development, image generation prompts, video scene storyboarding, voiceover scripting, music direction and Turkish caption writing with accurate timestamps.
Gemini — Google DeepMindUsed for AI image generation, producing the core campaign visuals including hero playground scenes, story moments and supporting assets based on Claude-developed prompts.
Veo 3 — Google DeepMindGenerated video footage and audio directly from text-based scene prompts, creating cinematic realistic scenes with synchronized audio.
Clipchamp — MicrosoftUsed for post-production editing, assembling scenes into a cohesive 60-second narrative, adding Turkish captions, timing adjustments and final export.
