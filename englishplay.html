Project Overview

What was built

The campaign was built entirely using AI tools — from creative strategy and scripting through to video generation and post-production. Claude developed the concept and scripted every scene. Gemini produced the hero visuals. Veo 3 generated cinematic footage with synchronized audio. Clipchamp assembled everything into a cohesive 60-second narrative ready for distribution. The result is a broadcast-ready advertisement delivered without a single camera, actor, or studio session.

Tools & Workflow

Production stack

Claude — Anthropic
Creative Engine

Primary creative engine for brand analysis, ad concept development, image generation prompts, video scene storyboarding, voiceover scripting, music direction, and Turkish caption writing with accurate timestamps.

Gemini — Google DeepMind
Image Generation

Used for AI image generation, producing core campaign visuals including hero playground scenes, story moments and supporting assets based on Claude-developed prompts.

Veo 3 — Google DeepMind
Video Generation

Generated video footage and audio directly from text-based scene prompts, creating cinematic realistic scenes with synchronized audio — no filming required.

Clipchamp — Microsoft
Post-Production

Used for post-production editing, assembling scenes into a cohesive 60-second narrative, adding Turkish captions, timing adjustments and final export.
