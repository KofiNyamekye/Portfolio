Lactadelle

Brand identity and a trade-partner website for a Ghanaian food startup making NutriPudding™ — a plant-based, shelf-stable pudding made from tiger nuts.

Overview

A new product, a new brand

Lactadelle makes NutriPudding™: a dairy-free pudding from tiger nuts and rice flour, packed in aseptic cartons that need no cold chain. The brand began as LactAfrica, with a milk-droplet-as-Africa mark and a cocoa, cream and gold palette, and evolved into Lactadelle.

The challenge

One site, many audiences

The website had to speak to retailers, distributors, schools and investors at the same time — and explain an unfamiliar product (why does a pudding keep for months without a fridge?) without overclaiming.

My approach

Facts first, then a clear next step

  1. PositioningLead with the product promise — “The pudding Africa deserves” — and back it immediately with four plain facts.
  2. StructureA focused one-page site: About, Product, How it’s made, FAQ and Contact.
  3. Explaining the productA four-step process (source, extract & blend, UHT, aseptic pack) shows why no refrigeration is needed.
  4. Partner conversion“Express interest” repeated where a buyer or investor would act, and a contact form that asks who is writing.
  5. Growth storyThe live product shown beside three “coming soon” lines, to show range without overpromising.

The result

A partner-ready brand presence

A live brand website that gives each type of partner a clear reason to get in touch and a clear way to do it.

Built for partners

Primary action is “Express interest”.

Facts before claims

Four short stats and a four-step process.

Audience-aware contact

Retailer, distributor, school or investor — routed from the start.

Answers up front

An expandable FAQ on product, storage, stocking and investment.

Share-ready

Open Graph metadata so links look right when sent to buyers.

Brand system

Mark, palette and type carried across site and packaging.
