The story

Ghanapiano sits where two dance-floor traditions meet: the melodic warmth of Ghanaian highlife and the groove of South African Amapiano. “Wo Ne Me” builds a single around that fusion and around its title idea — you and me. It shows the same AI-assisted workflow behind the rest of the Kofi Nyamekye catalogue adapting across genres: hiplife, hip-hop, Afropop and now Ghanapiano.
