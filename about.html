My story

From materials to digital experiences.

I’m Nyamekye Samuel Asamoah, a digital creative based in Ghana. I design websites, produce AI-powered video and content, create brand visuals and build data projects for individuals, businesses and organisations.

My foundation is engineering. I studied Materials Engineering at KNUST and trained in occupational health and safety. That background taught me to break problems down, test ideas and care about how things hold up in the real world.

Today I bring that mindset to creative and digital work. As Creative Director and AI tutor at CodeDevPay Academy, I lead campaign design and teach people to use AI tools well. I also release AI-assisted music as Kofi Nyamekye — a creative lab for the same video and storytelling skills I bring to client work.

I use AI the way a good studio uses any tool: under clear creative direction, with a person checking every result.
