The story

“Rise Again” is a Ghanaian hiplife and hip-hop testimony track. Its Twi hook — “mesɔre bio” — carries the whole message: I rise again. The release was treated as a campaign, not just a song drop. Alongside the audio, the package includes cover art and a matching thumbnail, a lyric video, an audio visualizer, a set of music video scenes and a three-clip TikTok teaser, so the song could land on every platform in a format that suits it.
